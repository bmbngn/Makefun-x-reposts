# Reposts for make.fun — five quotes and replies to @makefunai

Five posts from @Bensol1904 on X, each one a quote of, or a direct reply to, a different one of make.fun's newest posts — the GTA 6 results, the best-game winners, Slotbound's $1.33M week, the $1,666 open-world bounty, and the Mike Houser story — each carrying @makefunai and the make.fun play code in its own words.

Entry for [Get the most reposts and likes on make.fun's newest X posts](https://make.fun/bounty/makefun-x-reposts-02) on [make.fun](https://make.fun). Score is 2 points per repost and per quote plus 1 per like across all five posts; the top 50 share $500.

## Files

- `POSTS.json` — the addresses of the five X posts.
- `main.ts` — copies `POSTS.json` to the output directory.
